# Adidas US Product Dashboard

An Excel dashboard analysing 845 Adidas US product listings: pricing, discounts, ratings and the most reviewed products.

![Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-10-31%20225349.png)

## Tools
- Excel: pivot tables, charts, formulas

## What I did
- Checked the data: no duplicate SKUs, 16 blank original prices
- Fixed the discount formula and added department and price band columns
- Built 5 pivot tables and 5 charts
- Combined them into one dashboard

## Key findings
- Shoes are 50% of products (426 of 845) and cost the most, about $69 on average
- Women's and Men's products are about 75% of listings
- The average discount is 22.7%, fairly even across categories
- Accessories are rated highest (4.83), shoes lowest (4.54)
- The most reviewed products are all shoes: Stan Smith, Superstar and Adilette
